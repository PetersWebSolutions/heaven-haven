FAQ

Questions guests ask before they book

Click a question to read the answer.

Is it animal / pet friendly?

Yes. Leashed dogs and other well-behaved pets are welcome on the grounds and in most units. Please bring their own bed or crate, clean up after them, and keep them off the shared kitchen counters. Let us know in your booking notes so we can prepare a water bowl.

Do you have parking?

Yes — free parking is included with every stay. The road to the gate is concrete and sedan-friendly. The last 300 m is gravel. We have space for cars, vans and small groups; no need to reserve a slot unless you’re arriving with more than four vehicles.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out depends on availability — message us the day before. Day tours run 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM at ₱250 per head.

How far is it from Metro Manila?

About 2 hours via Marcos Highway (Marikina–Infanta Road). Turn at the Cuyambay junction and follow the concrete road for 3.5 km. We’re in Sitio Kaybuto, Brgy. Cuyambay, Tanay, Rizal. Send us a message after you book and we’ll share the pin.
